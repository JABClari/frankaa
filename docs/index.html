frankaa

said FRAN-kah

A photo slider that follows your finger, and a lightbox that unfurls each photo from its spot.

$ npm install frankaa

procession the slider

Swipe, scroll or use the arrow keys. Click a photo to open it.

unfurl the lightbox

Open a photo, move with the arrows or a swipe, and close with Escape or a pull down.

Showing with WebGL

Use it

Plain TypeScript with no dependencies. React bindings are optional.

Built in

  • Native scroll snap: the swipe is 1:1 with the finger and keeps its momentum.
  • Photos past the first load only when the slider comes near the screen.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys, Home and End in the slider; arrows, Tab and Escape in the lightbox.
  • The lightbox keeps focus inside and gives it back when it closes.
  • Reduced motion turns the spring and the crossfade into a short fade.
  • No WebGL, or a photo it cannot draw: the same lightbox shows a plain image.
